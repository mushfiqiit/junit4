B) Cause of the warning

The warning occurs at the `super(next, befores, null);` call in `RunBeforeParams`. NullAway treats unannotated constructor parameters as `@Nonnull` by default, so passing the `null` literal to the third `RunBefores` constructor parameter is considered unsafe.
