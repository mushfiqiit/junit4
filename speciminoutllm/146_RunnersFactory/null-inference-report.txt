B) Cause of the warning

The warning is caused by the assignment `runnerOverride = assumptionViolationRunner;` inside the `RunnersFactory` constructor. The local variable `assumptionViolationRunner` is initialized to `null` and only assigned a non-null runner in the `catch` block, so NullAway treats the right-hand side as `@Nullable`. The field `runnerOverride` is unannotated, so NullAway treats it as `@NonNull` by default, making the assignment unsafe. Annotating `runnerOverride` as `@Nullable` tells NullAway that the field is allowed to hold `null`, which resolves this warning.
