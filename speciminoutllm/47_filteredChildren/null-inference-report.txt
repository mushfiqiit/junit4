

## A) Annotation decisions

| location | inferred annotation | one-sentence reason |
|---|---:|---|
| `ParentRunner.filteredChildren` | `@Nullable` | The field is explicitly initialized to `null` and represents a lazily populated cache, so it is intended to be nullable. |

## B) Cause of the warning

The warning points to the initializer `null` in `private volatile List<T> filteredChildren = null;`. Under NullAway’s default assumptions, the unannotated field `List<T> filteredChildren` is `@NonNull`, while the literal `null` is a `@Nullable` expression. Therefore NullAway reports that a `@Nullable` expression is being assigned to a `@NonNull` field. Annotating `filteredChildren` as `@Nullable` makes the field’s declared nullness match its actual usage and allows the null initializer.
