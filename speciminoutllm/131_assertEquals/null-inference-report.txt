### B) Cause of the warning

The warning occurs on line 10 at the call `assertEquals(null, expected, actual, delta)`. NullAway treats the unannotated `String message` parameter of the 4-argument `assertEquals` method as `@NonNull` by default, but the 3-argument overload passes the `null` literal for it. Marking the `message` parameter `@Nullable` tells NullAway that this parameter may receive `null`, which resolves the warning.
