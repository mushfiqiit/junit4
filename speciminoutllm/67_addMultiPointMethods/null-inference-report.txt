

## A) Annotation decisions

| location | inferred annotation | one-sentence reason |
|---|---:|---|
| `org.junit.runners.model.FrameworkMethod.invokeExplosively(...)` — first parameter `Object target` | `@Nullable` | This parameter may be `null` when invoking a static method, and the reported warning comes from explicitly passing `null` as the `target` argument. |

## B) Cause of the warning

The warning is caused by the `null` literal passed to `dataPointsMethod.invokeExplosively(null)` in `AllMembersSupplier.java`. Under NullAway, the unannotated `Object target` parameter of `FrameworkMethod.invokeExplosively` is treated as `@NonNull`, so passing a `null` literal is unsafe. Since `FrameworkMethod.invokeExplosively` can legitimately be invoked with a `null` target for static methods, annotating the `target` parameter as `@Nullable` resolves the warning.

      throws Throwable {
