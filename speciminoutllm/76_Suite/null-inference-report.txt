## B) Cause of the warning

The warning points to the `null` literal passed as the first argument to `builder.runners(null, classes)` on line 13 of `Suite.java`. Since `RunnerBuilder.runners` has no nullness annotations, NullAway assumes its `parent` parameter is `@NonNull` by default, so passing the literal `null` is reported as unsafe. Annotating `parent` with `@Nullable` tells NullAway that this parameter may receive `null`, which resolves the warning. The other `null` literal in `this(null, ...)` is a separate potential warning and is intentionally left unchanged.
