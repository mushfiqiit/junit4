B) Cause of the warning

At `ComparisonFailure.java:13`, the call passes `super.getMessage()`, which NullAway recognizes as `@Nullable` because it ultimately comes from `Throwable.getMessage()`, to the unannotated `@NonNull` parameter of `ComparisonCompactor.compact(String message)`. Annotating that parameter as `@Nullable` makes the nullability of the argument and parameter compatible.
