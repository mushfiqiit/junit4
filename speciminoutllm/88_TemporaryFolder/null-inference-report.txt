B) Cause of the warning

The warning is caused by assigning the nullable field `builder.parentFolder` to the outer field `this.parentFolder`. NullAway treats the unannotated outer `File parentFolder` field as `@NonNull`, while `Builder.parentFolder` is explicitly annotated `@Nullable`. Therefore, the assignment is considered unsafe. Annotating the outer `TemporaryFolder#parentFolder` field as `@Nullable` makes the assignment type-correct and matches the intended JUnit 4 behavior where the parent folder may be omitted.
