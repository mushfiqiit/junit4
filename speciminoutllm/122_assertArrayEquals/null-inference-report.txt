B) Cause of the warning

The warning occurs at the call `assertArrayEquals(null, expecteds, actuals);` inside the two-argument `assertArrayEquals` method. NullAway treats the unannotated `String message` parameter of the three-argument overload as `@NonNull` by default, so passing the `null` literal is unsafe. Annotating `message` as `@Nullable` tells NullAway that this parameter is allowed to be `null`, which resolves the reported warning.
