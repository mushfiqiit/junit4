B) Cause of the warning

The warning is reported at the delegating constructor call `this(assumption, false, null, null);`. NullAway treats unannotated object-typed parameters as `@Nonnull`, so passing the `null` literals for `Object value` and `Matcher<?> matcher` is considered unsafe. Marking those two formal parameters `@Nullable` makes the `null` arguments acceptable and resolves the warning without changing any runtime behavior.
