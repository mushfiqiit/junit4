## B) Cause of the warning

The warning is triggered on line 19 of `ParentRunner.java`, where `withBeforeClasses` returns `new RunBefores(statement, befores, null)`. The third argument, `null`, is passed to the `target` parameter of `RunBefores`. Since `RunBefores` has no nullness annotations, NullAway assumes its `Object target` parameter is `@NonNull` by default, so passing a literal `null` is reported as unsafe. Annotating `target` as `@Nullable` declares that this constructor argument may be null, matching the actual JUnit usage and resolving the warning.
