## B) Cause of the warning

The warning points to the declaration of `getThreadCpuTimeMethod` on line 10 of `ReflectiveThreadMXBean.java`. Under NullAway’s default assumptions, an unannotated `static final Method` field is treated as `@Nonnull`, so NullAway expects it to be definitely initialized with a non-null value. Therefore, the assignment `getThreadCpuTimeMethod = threadCpuTime;` may assign `null` to what NullAway considers a non-null static final field, producing the “not initialized” warning. Annotating the field `@Nullable` resolves this by explicitly allowing the field to hold `null`.
