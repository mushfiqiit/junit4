B) Cause of the warning

The warning is caused by the expression `method.invokeExplosively(null)` on line 17 of `AllMembersSupplier.java`. NullAway treats the first parameter of `FrameworkMethod.invokeExplosively`, `Object target`, as `@NonNull` because JUnit 4 has no nullability annotations. Passing the literal `null` to that unannotated parameter therefore triggers `[NullAway] passing @Nullable parameter 'null' where @NonNull is required`. Annotating `target` as `@Nullable` correctly reflects that it may be null when invoking static methods, resolving the warning.
