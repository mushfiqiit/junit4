B) Cause of the warning

The warning is triggered by the `null` literal in `assertNotNull(null, object)` inside the one-argument overload. NullAway treats unannotated method parameters as `@Nonnull` by default, so the `String message` parameter of the two-argument `assertNotNull` is assumed to require a non-null value. Passing the `null` literal to it is therefore considered unsafe. Annotating `message` as `@Nullable` explicitly states that the message may be absent, which allows the `null` literal and fixes the warning without changing program logic.
