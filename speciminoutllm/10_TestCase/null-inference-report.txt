

## A) Annotation decisions

| location | inferred annotation | one-sentence reason |
|---|---|---|
| `junit.framework.TestCase.fName` | `@Nullable` | The constructor assigns `null` to `fName`, so the field is allowed to hold a null value. |

## B) Cause of the warning

The warning is reported on the assignment `fName = null;` inside `TestCase`’s constructor. NullAway treats unannotated fields as `@Nonnull` by default, so assigning the null literal to `fName` is considered unsafe. Annotating `fName` with `@Nullable` tells NullAway that this field is allowed to be null, which resolves the warning.
