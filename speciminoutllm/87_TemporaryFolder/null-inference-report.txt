## B) Cause of the warning

The warning is caused by the assignment `this.parentFolder = parentFolder;` in the constructor. NullAway treats the field `parentFolder` as `@NonNull` by default, but the right-hand side is a `@Nullable` constructor parameter. Marking the field `@Nullable` tells NullAway that the field is allowed to hold a null value, matching the actual API behavior and resolving the assignment warning.
