B) Cause of the warning

The warning is on the call `assertArrayEquals(null, expecteds, actuals)` in the two-argument `assertArrayEquals` method. The first argument is a literal `null`, but the `String message` parameter of the three-argument overload is unannotated and therefore treated by NullAway as `@NonNull`. Annotating `message` as `@Nullable` tells NullAway that this parameter is allowed to receive `null`, which resolves the warning.
