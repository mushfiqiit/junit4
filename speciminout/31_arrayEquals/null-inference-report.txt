## B) Cause of the warning

The warning is reported at the call to the private `arrayEquals(message, expecteds, actuals, true)` overload. The public method declares `message` as `@Nullable`, but the private overload declares its `message` parameter without an annotation, so NullAway treats it as `@NonNull`. Therefore, passing the nullable `message` value to the private method is considered unsafe. Annotating the private `message` parameter with `@Nullable` matches its actual null-handling behavior and resolves the warning.
