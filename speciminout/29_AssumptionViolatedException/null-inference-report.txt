B) Cause of the warning

The warning points to the assignment of the constructor parameter `value` to the field `fValue`. `value` is annotated `@Nullable`, but `fValue` has no nullness annotation, so NullAway treats it as non-null by default. Storing a possibly-null value into an unannotated `Object` field is therefore unsafe. Marking `fValue` as `@Nullable` records the actual invariant that this field may be null and resolves the warning without changing program logic.
