## B) Cause of the warning

The warning occurs at the call:

```java
putField.put("fValue", SerializableValueDescription.asSerializableValue(fValue));
```

The field `fValue` is annotated `@Nullable`, but the parameter `value` in `SerializableValueDescription.asSerializableValue(Object value)` has no nullness annotation. Under NullAway’s default assumptions, unannotated parameters are `@NonNull`, so passing the possibly-null `fValue` into that method is reported as unsafe. Annotating `value` as `@Nullable` resolves the warning by telling NullAway that the method is allowed to receive a null value.
